# Week 2 - Rebuild LinkedIn

## Outcome

Turn your LinkedIn profile from a list of titles into a clear professional story.

## Do the work

Use the [LinkedIn profile worksheet](../templates/linkedin-profile.md) and update in this order:

1. **Headline**: make your direction readable in one line.
2. **About**: explain your interests, evidence, and what you are exploring.
3. **Featured**: add your strongest project, proof, and post.
4. **Experience**: replace responsibility lists with context, action, outcome, and evidence.
5. **Visual scan**: check that a stranger can understand you in 20 seconds.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Headline reflects your current direction
- [ ] About section has a story and evidence
- [ ] At least two Featured links work
- [ ] One experience or project entry has been rewritten

## Submit

Share your profile link or a redacted draft. Ask: *What would someone remember about me after scanning this?*
