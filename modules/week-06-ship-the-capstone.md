# Week 6 - Ship the capstone

## Outcome

Publish a case study that makes your process and proof easy to share.

## Do the work

1. Pick one project that best represents your direction.
2. Complete the [capstone case study template](../templates/capstone-case-study.md).
3. Record a 90-second introduction using the [pitch guide](../templates/ninety-second-pitch.md).
4. Publish the case study in a repository, portfolio, or public post.
5. Decide a sustainable weekly sharing habit: one post, one reflection, or one project update.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A public or shareable case study
- [ ] A 90-second pitch you can deliver naturally
- [ ] A link to evidence, demo, or repository
- [ ] A realistic weekly publishing habit

## Final reflection

Answer in five sentences: What changed in your story? What proof did you make visible? What did you share? What will you keep doing? What is your next build?
