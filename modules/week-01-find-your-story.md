# Week 1 - Find your story

## Outcome

Write a one-line positioning statement grounded in real evidence.

## Why this matters

Titles describe a moment. A story connects your interests, skills, and direction so people can understand what you are building toward.

## Do the work

1. List 8-10 moments when you made, improved, explained, researched, led, or learned something.
2. Circle the moments that gave you energy.
3. Attach proof to each: a project, result, screenshot, feedback, or lesson.
4. Use the [positioning statement worksheet](../templates/positioning-statement.md) to draft three versions.
5. Choose the version that is specific, believable, and useful as a compass.

## Definition of done

- [ ] One positioning statement in plain language
- [ ] Three pieces of evidence behind it
- [ ] One project or artifact you will strengthen next

## Submit

Share your statement, your evidence links, and ask: *What feels clear and what still feels generic?*
