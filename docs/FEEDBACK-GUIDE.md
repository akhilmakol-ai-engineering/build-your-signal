# Feedback guide

Good feedback is specific, kind, and useful.

## The signal check

1. **Clear**: Can I understand what this person is interested in or building?
2. **Credible**: Is there evidence behind the claim?
3. **Concrete**: Can I see what they made, changed, learned, or decided?
4. **Connected**: Do LinkedIn, GitHub, and content tell a consistent story?

## Useful feedback format

```text
I noticed...
The strongest proof is...
One place I got confused is...
The smallest high-impact next step is...
```

Avoid vague praise, personal comparisons, and requests for private information.
