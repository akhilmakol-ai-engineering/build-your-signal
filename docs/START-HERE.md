# Start here

Welcome. This course is not about looking impressive. It is about making your potential visible.

## Before Week 1

Create a working space in your fork: `drafts/`, `evidence/`, and `links.md`.

Keep private material private. Link to public work where possible; redact company, client, academic, or personal information when needed.

## Your weekly rhythm

1. **Learn**: read the module and its definition of done.
2. **Make**: use the matching template in `templates/`.
3. **Publish**: update the relevant public profile, repository, or post.
4. **Reflect**: write down what changed and what you learned.
5. **Request feedback**: submit an issue with links and one focused question.

## How to submit work

Use the **Weekly submission** issue template in the course repository. Include the week, public links or a redacted draft, a short reflection, and one concrete question. Feedback focuses on clarity, evidence, and next steps - not perfection.

## A note on authenticity

Use AI as an editor, sparring partner, or idea generator. Do not publish invented work, copied claims, or a voice that does not sound like you. Your signal becomes credible when every statement can be backed by evidence.
