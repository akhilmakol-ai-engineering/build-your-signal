# 90-second pitch

1. **Who I am** - "I'm [name], a [current identity] interested in [area]."
2. **What I care about** - "I am especially interested in [problem or audience] because [reason]."
3. **Proof** - "Recently, I built [project]. I [action], which led to [outcome or learning]."
4. **Direction** - "Next, I am looking to learn or contribute in [direction]."
5. **Invitation** - "I'd love to connect with people working on [topic]."
