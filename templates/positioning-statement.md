## Draft the sentence

`I am a [student / early-career builder] interested in [area]. I use [skills] to help [people / teams / problems] by [kind of contribution].`

### Examples

- I am a computer science student interested in reliable systems. I use Python and data analysis to turn messy operations problems into simple tools.
- I am a design student interested in accessible digital products. I use research and prototyping to make complex tasks easier to understand.

## Final version

Write one sentence you can stand behind today, then name one project that makes it more believable.
