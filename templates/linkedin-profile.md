# LinkedIn profile worksheet

## Headline

`[Current identity] | [skill or domain] | Building toward [direction]`

## About

Write four short paragraphs:

1. What interests you and why
2. The problems you enjoy
3. Two or three pieces of evidence
4. A clear invitation to connect

## Featured

- Strongest project repository
- Case study, demo, or portfolio item
- One post that shows your voice

## Experience and projects

For each entry: **context -> action -> outcome -> evidence**.
